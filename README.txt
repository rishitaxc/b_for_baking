B FOR BAKING WEBSITE

OPEN AND PUBLISH
Open index.html in a browser, keeping all accompanying files in place.
To publish, upload index.html, styles.css, app.js, gallery-data.js, builder.js, baker-bot.js, photo-sources.json and the assets folder to a static website host. No build step is required.

CUSTOM CAKE BUILDER
Customers can choose round/heart/square shapes, one to three tiers, frosting and decoration colours, flowers/pearls/sprinkles/minimal decoration, a flavour wish, a personal message and extra design notes. A live illustration shows the choices. Heart shapes use one tier. The illustration can be downloaded as a PNG. Using the design saves a snapshot to the order form. Design preferences are included as text in WhatsApp; customers may attach the saved picture themselves. The illustration is a brief, not a guarantee of the final cake's appearance or available flavours.

WEIGHT ESTIMATE
Guests are multiplied by a chosen 100 g, 125 g or 150 g portion allowance, rounded up to the next 0.5 kg. These are planning assumptions, not bakery serving guarantees. Actual servings depend on recipe, filling, design and cutting. The bakery confirms final weight. Hampers do not require a cake-weight estimate.
