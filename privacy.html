Your information.

When you submit an enquiry, B for Baking saves your name, phone number, celebration date, design preferences, guest count and other information you choose to provide. The bakery uses these details to respond to your request and manage your order. Only the signed-in owner can view the dashboard.

The website counts page views and clicks to Instagram. A random browser cookie helps count unique browsers over 30 days. Analytics do not contain your name, phone number, Instagram identity or IP address. Clearing cookies or using another device may count the same person again.

An Instagram click is counted when the website redirects you to Instagram; it does not confirm that Instagram loaded, or that you followed the account. Instagram and WhatsApp apply their own privacy practices after you leave this website.

Customers can check their enquiry status using the enquiry reference and the full phone number supplied with it. The tracker shows only the reference, status and update time, without contact details or private bakery notes. Keep your reference private. Quiz answers stay in your browser until you choose a design and include them in an enquiry.

The website does not collect card details or process payments. Optional WhatsApp follow-ups are sent only when you open WhatsApp and send a message yourself.

To ask about your enquiry information, contact the bakery at +91 70052 46168. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in design notes.
